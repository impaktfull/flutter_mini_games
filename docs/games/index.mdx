## Puzzles

Single player, turn based. See [Puzzles](/games/puzzles).

| Game | Builder | Id | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trail | `TrailGameBuilder` | `trail` | Draw one trail through every cell, passing the numbered checkpoints in order |
| Sudoku | `SudokuGameBuilder` | `sudoku` | Fill the 9x9 grid so every row, column and box contains 1 to 9 |
| Minesweeper | `MinesweeperGameBuilder` | `minesweeper` | Reveal every safe cell without hitting a mine |
| 2048 | `TwentyFortyEightGameBuilder` | `twenty_forty_eight` | Slide the tiles and merge equal numbers until you reach 2048 |
| Switch | `SwitchGameBuilder` | `switch_lights` | Turn off all the lights, a tap toggles a light and its neighbours |
| Code | `CodeGameBuilder` | `code` | Crack the hidden code of 4 symbols in 8 tries |
| Pairs | `PairsGameBuilder` | `pairs` | Flip two cards at a time and find all matching pairs |

## Keyboard

Games work with touch first. Where it makes sense they also take a keyboard: arrows or WASD to swipe or steer, space or enter to tap, jump or drop.
