---
title: Two players
description: Rules and results of the two-player games played on one phone.
---

Two players share one phone, sitting opposite each other: **Player 1** holds the phone (bottom, `primary` color) and **Player 2** sits across (top, `secondary` color). The top half is rotated so both players read their side. Ownership is never shown by color alone: players also have their own symbol or pattern.

All two-player games end with `GameOutcome.won` and `data['winner']` (`'bottom'` or `'top'`), or `GameOutcome.draw`. They have no score.

## Turn based

Players take turns on one shared board. A banner on each side shows whose turn it is.

### Tic Tac Toe

Take turns as X (Player 1, starts) and O. Three in a row wins, a full board is a draw.

### Boxes

Dots and boxes on a 4x4 grid. Take turns drawing a line between two dots. Close the fourth side of a box to claim it and draw again. Most boxes wins. `data`: `boxes` (per player).

### Four Up

Take turns dropping a disc in one of 7 columns. Four in a row (horizontal, vertical or diagonal) wins, a full grid is a draw. Also arrow keys to select a column and arrow down to drop.

### Flip

Reversi on an 8x8 board. Place a disc so it outflanks discs of your opponent: those flip to your color. A player without a legal move passes. Most discs when neither can move wins. `data`: `discs` (per player).

## Real time

Real-time duels are matches of rounds: both players tap "ready", a 3-2-1 countdown starts every round, and the first to win enough rounds wins the match. Both players can touch the screen at the same time. `data['rounds']` holds the rounds won per player.

### Puck

Air hockey. Drag your mallet in your own half and shoot the puck into the other goal. First to 5 goals.

```dart
PuckGameBuilder(goalsToWin: 7)
```

### Quickdraw

Best of 5. After a random wait the signal shows "GO!". The first to tap their half wins the round, tapping before the signal loses it. `data`: `bestReactionMs` (per player).

### Push

Best of 3, sumo style. Tap your half to push your disc toward the other one. Where you tap aims the push. The ring shrinks until a disc is out.

### Slap

First to 5 points. The attacker slaps or fakes, the defender holds the target and pulls away in time. Pull away from a slap and the defender scores, flinch at a fake or get slapped and the attacker scores. Roles swap every round.

### Snatch

First to 7 points. A symbol appears in the middle: be the first to tap it in your half. A wrong tap costs a point and locks your half for a moment. `data`: `points`, `penalties`.

### Tug

Best of 3 tug of war. Tap your half as fast as you can to pull the marker to your side.
